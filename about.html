About UniShare

A simple student marketplace for donating, renting and selling unused academic items.

What is UniShare?

A website where university students share books, electronics, notes, lab items and project materials with each other.

Why it was created

Students spend a lot on items they use for one semester, while many useful things sit unused. UniShare connects the two.

Main objectives

Encourage resource sharing, reduce student expenses, minimise waste and build a helpful campus community.

How students benefit

Find affordable resources, earn from unused items, and help juniors and classmates.

How Donate, Rent and Sell Work

Donate

Free items. Contact the lister to arrange collection.

Rent

Pay a daily or weekly rate (plus optional deposit). Contact the lister to arrange borrowing and return.

Sell

Fixed price. Contact the lister to negotiate or arrange collection.

Prototype limits: listings are saved only in your own browser (localStorage). Real shared listings, private messaging and secure accounts would need a backend in a future version.
